# Tristen Chen | Mechanical & Hardware Engineering Portfolio

Welcome to the source code for my personal engineering portfolio.

## About Me
I am a Mechanical Engineering student (B.A.Sc.) at the University of Toronto, actively seeking PEY Co-op opportunities. I specialize in designing, simulating, and manufacturing robotics and optomechanical systems. 

This portfolio showcases my work in:
- Soft Robotics & Kinematics
- CAD, GD&T, and Mechanical Assembly (SolidWorks)
- Numerical Analysis & Thermal Modeling (MATLAB)
- Embedded Systems & Prototyping (C/C++, ESP32)

## Built With
This website was custom-built from scratch without website builders (like Wix or Squarespace) to ensure a lightweight, fast, and fully responsive experience.
- **HTML5** (Single-page architecture)
- **Tailwind CSS** (Styling and drafting-board aesthetic)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Modal galleries and interactivity)
