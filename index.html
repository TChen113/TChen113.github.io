Trace: Soft Robotics Telepresence System

April 2026 - Present | Project Founder

> Project Overview

Let's see if I can summarize this in two sentences:

Trace is a physical gift for someone you love but can only interact with through a screen. It is a plush, abstract robotic companion that translates your body language during a video call into gentle, real-time motion so they can feel you miles away.

Most robotic arms use multiple localized joints to achieve 5+ DOF, which makes them heavy and expensive. I wanted the arm to be lighter and softer while using fewer motors, so I chose horizontal sweeping and retracting motion through compliant joints combined with up-and-down rotation. This allows me to increase the arm's x-y plane mobility with only 2 DOF.

MIE235 Heat Transfer and Liquid Contact Angle Solver

Feb 2026 - April 2026 | Individual Coursework

> Project Overview

Project 1: The objective of this project was to evaluate the convective cooling efficiency of a triangular aluminum heatsink fin subjected to a constant thermal load. The core goal was to mathematically model the fin's thermal behavior to predict its internal temperature distribution and calculate the absolute minimum convective heat transfer coefficient required by a cooling fan to keep the component safely below its 930K melting point.

Project 2: The objective of this project was to determine the hydrophobic properties of a surface by mathematically analyzing experimental image data of a liquid droplet. The project required transforming raw coordinate data into continuous mathematical models to accurately calculate the droplet's contact angle, total volume, and the Eötvös number to assess the balance between surface tension and gravitational body forces.

> Gallery

Fig 1. Droplet contact angle analysis under different degrees of polynomial fitting

Fig 2. Fin Temperature Distribution based on different numbers of fins sections (n)

Fig 3. Hand derivation of the heat balance equation for the middle sections of the cooling fin

> Engineering Details

  • Discretized a 1D steady-state thermal model for an aluminum heatsink fin using a finite volume formulation.
  • Engineered a custom MATLAB solver using the Thomas Algorithm (TDMA) to resolve tridiagonal matrix systems.
  • Implemented a Secant method root-finding algorithm to calculate the minimum convective heat transfer coefficient required to prevent component melting.
  • Programmed a least-squares polynomial regression algorithm in MATLAB to model droplet boundary profiles, evaluating 2nd through 9th order fits to optimize the coefficient of determination (R^2) while preventing endpoint distortion.
  • Estimated surface hydrophobicity by calculating contact angles from derivative slopes at the liquid-solid boundary, and computed the Eötvös number to evaluate surface tension versus gravity.
  • Implemented multi-segment Trapezoidal and Simpson's 1/3 rules to numerically integrate the fitted polynomials and accurately estimate total droplet volumes.
